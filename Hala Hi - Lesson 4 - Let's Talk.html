Lesson Goals

What you'll practice today

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  • 1 Talk about your daily routine.
  • 2 Describe what you are doing right now.
  • 3 Build correct sentences using both rules.
  • 4 Take part in short conversations.
  • 5 Gain more confidence speaking English.
1 · Ice Breaker

🎲 Word Chain

⏱️ 5 minutes
👥 Whole class

The teacher starts with a verb. Each student says a new verb — no repeats!

study → play → read → drive → exercise → eat → travel → listen → ...

Rules

  • Verbs only.
  • No repeating a word.
  • 5 seconds to answer.
  • The last student standing wins.

3 · Speed Challenge

⚡ Habit or Now?

⏱️ 10 minutes
✍️ Write in chat

The teacher shows a verb for 5 seconds. Students write one sentence for each rule.

Current verb
cook
Habit — Present SimpleI cook every weekend.
Now — Present ContinuousI am cooking dinner now.
4 · What's Changing?

🎯 Spot the Change

⏱️ 8 minutes
👀 Observation

Look at the scene. The teacher describes it, then changes one detail. Say what's different!

He is reading. → Now he is drinking coffee.
The woman is sitting. → Now she is standing.
The boy is eating. → Now he is playing.

6 · Pass the Question

🎤 Pass the Question

⏱️ 8 minutes
🔁 Chain activity

The teacher starts with a question. Each student answers, then asks a new question to another student.

Teacher: What do you usually do after work?
Student 1: I usually watch YouTube.
Student 1: Ahmed, what are you doing these days?
Ahmed: I'm learning to drive...

Continue the chain until everyone has spoken.

7 · Guess Who?

🕵️ Guess Who?

⏱️ 5 minutes
🧩 Description game

One student picks a person in the picture and describes them. The rest of the class guesses who it is.

He is wearing a blue shirt.
He is drinking coffee.
He is sitting near the window.

9 · Final Speaking Challenge

🎙️ Talk About Yourself

⏱️ 30–45 seconds each

Speak about yourself using both grammar rules. Cover these points:

Your name
Your job or studies
What you do every day
What you're doing these days
A hobby you enjoy
Why you're learning English
