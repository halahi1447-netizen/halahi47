Lesson Goals

What you'll learn today

By the end of this lesson, you will be able to:

  • 1 Use am / is / are with confidence.
  • 2 Describe people, places, and things.
  • 3 Build longer, more natural sentences.
  • 4 Start using natural conversational English.

Story

A Flight to Canada

Omar is at King Abdulaziz International Airport.

Today is a special day.

He is excited because this is his first international trip.

His parents are with him.

His mother is a little nervous, but his father is calm.

Story (continued)

Everything is ready

Their flight is at 9:30.

The airport is busy because many people are traveling.

The information desk is near Gate 12.

The employees are very helpful.

Omar's passport is in his backpack. His suitcase is ready.

Everything is ready for the trip.

He smiles and says: "I am ready."

Notice the Pattern

Look closely — what do you notice?

Tap each sentence to think about why we used that word

I am ready. "I" always uses am▾
Omar is excited. One person → is▾
His parents are with him. More than one → are▾
The airport is busy. One place/thing → is▾
The employees are helpful. Plural people → are▾
Grammar

The verb "To Be"

I
am
  • I'm ready.
  • I'm tired.
  • I'm hungry.
  • I'm at work.
He / She / It
is
  • My father is busy.
  • Your teacher is here.
  • The meeting is today.
  • His phone is new.
You / We / They
are
  • Your parents are outside.
  • The students are ready.
  • Our neighbors are friendly.
  • These books are expensive.
