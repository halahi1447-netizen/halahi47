🔗 Warm-up Game

Word Chain

The teacher starts with a word. Each student says a word that begins with the last letter of the previous word.

📋 Game Rules

How to Play

🚫 No repeating words.
⏱️ You have only 5 seconds.
👋 If you don't know a word, you're out of the round.
🏆 The last person standing wins.

💡 At the end, ask students the meaning of a few new words.

🏆 Final Challenge

Go Beyond One Sentence

Pick 3 questions from earlier. Speak for 30–45 seconds on each — no one-word or one-sentence answers!

❌ Too short "What did you do yesterday?"

I stayed home.
✅ Full answer "What did you do yesterday?"

Yesterday I stayed home because I wasn't feeling very well. I watched two episodes of a TV series, ordered dinner online, and talked to my cousin on the phone. Before going to bed, I read a few pages of an English book.
